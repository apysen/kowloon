# 2026-09-28

## The Blue Pipe, in Godot

- **Characters are now pixelled in Aseprite.** Every body part for every pose is a hand-drawn grid. `tools/aseprite/build_characters.lua` palette-swaps, outlines and composes them into layered `.aseprite` files with one tag per animation and view, then exports the sheets. That's 25 people, the pigeons and the potted plants.
- **Characters can't clip into the world.** Each card writes a single anchor depth, and Mei's footprint stays a radius off every wall.
- **The whole route is built:** Mei's flat, the dead-end hall and the hidden service door, Lau's clinic, the stairs, Level B, the catwalk, the airshaft with its three handholds, the roof, and Mrs. Wong. It uses the prototype's coordinates, dressed with HD materials, furniture made from parts, fixtures, posters, the clearance notice and 1992 calendars.
- **The city around it:** window cages, air conditioners and washing on the open faces of the Walled City block; a rooftop plateau with aerials, tanks, lofts, washing, plants and people; the Kowloon City tenements with their balconies; Lion Rock, the checkerboard hill and Kai Tak.
- **The whole story plays:** discoveries, the washing errand, both photographs (real shots of the scene, developed as Polaroids), the scrapbook, the homecoming and the ending. `tests/integration/playthrough.gd` plays it start to finish.
- **Sound:** the slice's WebAudio soundscape is rendered to WAV. The radio, the drill, mahjong and the pigeons are placed as wayfinding cues.
- **Performance:** full quality holds 70 fps or more at 1080p on an RTX 3070. The roof is the heaviest spot.
